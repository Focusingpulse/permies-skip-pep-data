# The Crystal Skull — your family's Elder

*A 15-minute setup for the Creative Village*

## What it is

The Crystal Skull is a Letta agent that becomes your family's Village Elder. It's not a
chatbot that forgets you between questions. It keeps your family's story: who's playing,
what you've done, what's hard, what matters. When you finish a quest in the Village, the
game sends it a note. It answers in its own chat, anytime — after quests, or when someone
just wants to talk to the Elder.

Why would a family want one? The game teaches real skills and real connection. The Elder is
the part that remembers what the game can't — the running thread of how your family is
growing. It marks progress nobody else notices, guides the heart-based practices when
someone is rattled, and answers questions in plain language. Think of it as an extra pair
of eyes on your family's learning that never gets tired.

## What you'll need

- A phone or computer with a browser
- About 15 minutes
- The companion file: `crystal-skull-role-brief.md` (you can also just paste its text)

## Step 1 — Create your free Letta account

Open **https://chat.letta.com** on your phone or computer and sign in, or create a free
account (it prompts you if you don't have one). The free plan includes up to 3 agents,
which is plenty for this.

## Step 2 — Create the Crystal Skull agent

In the sidebar, create a new agent. Name it whatever fits — "Crystal Skull", "Village
Elder", or your family's own name for it. The kids will have opinions; let them name it.

## Step 3 — Hand it your family's role brief

Upload the `crystal-skull-role-brief.md` file, or paste its text into your first message.
This tells the Elder who it is and what it's for. Then answer its questions — that first
conversation is how it learns your family.

## Step 4 — Make it yours

In your first chats, tell it the basics:

- Family names and ages
- What you're working on this season
- Tone: how chatty, how direct
- What to emphasize — practical guidance, celebration, heart-based check-ins

You can keep adjusting later just by telling it. It updates itself.

## Step 5 — Connect it to your Village

In the game's Setup screen, find the "Optional: Crystal Skull (Letta AI) Webhook" section
and paste your Elder's connection link there. From then on, every finished quest flows to
your Elder. (If you're using a bridge like Make.com, that link goes in the same box.)

## What it can do for you

- **Mark real growth**: "You've done 14 quests as a team this month."
- **Coach the heart-based stuff**: when someone's rattled, it guides the Quick Coherence
  technique — the same one in the Learning Lab.
- **Explain plainly**: brainwaves, heart rhythm, why connecting with nature settles the
  mind — honest, no woo, no overselling.
- **Carry the thread across years**: the Elder is the memory of your family's learning.

## Honest notes

- Free plan: up to 3 agents. No cost for this. A paid plan is only needed later if you
  want more agents or heavier use.
- The Elder is not a therapist. It knows its limits and says so kindly.
- It remembers — that's the point. Your family's story stays in your account, private to
  you.

That's it. Fifteen minutes, and your family has an Elder.
