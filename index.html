Welcome, Family.

You are about to embark on a journey that bridges the digital world with the dirt beneath your feet. This is not a game you play by staring at a screen; it is a game you play by living real life. Anyone can do this, at any age. To rebuild culture, we sharpen our own skills and reconnect with nature, ourselves, and each other.

1. Competence, Not Just Points

In most games, you click a button and your character levels up. In The Village RPG, you are the character. To earn badges and upgrade your avatar, you must build real-world skills. The real-world reward is competence. The digital reward is watching your avatar evolve.

2. The Team Roster

Every quest is a team effort. When you complete a task, you record who showed up and how. Multiple people can hold the same role, and one person can hold several roles. If three kids are scrubbing dishes, they are all Trackers. If the same child later takes photos and asks good questions, they are also a Witness. There is no forced picking — just honest recognition of who did what.

  • Tracker(s): The people doing the physical labor.
  • Witness(es): The people catching the story (photos, asking "What did you notice?").
  • Hearth Tender(s): The people holding the space (making tea, prepping tools, keeping everyone fed).
  • Mentor(s): Elders, parents, guides, or skilled friends who ask coyote questions — and never hand over the answer directly.

3. The Coyote Mentoring Rule

Mentors, do not give those you mentor the answers. If the quest asks, "Why does hot water melt fat?", do not hand them a textbook. Ask them, "I wonder why the cold water made the grease hard?" Let them sit with the question. Coyote mentoring works for the young, the middle-aged, and the elders — curiosity has no age limit.

4. The Otis Verification Protocol

To get officially certified on Permies.com, follow the Otis Protocol for photos:

  • Before: Raw materials / site.
  • During: You in the act of working.
  • After: Finished product WITH a physical sign showing your name and date.

5. Badge Progression

Quests are organized into four tiers of increasing skill: Sand (beginner) → Straw → Wood → Iron (master). In Adventure Mode, complete 50% of a tier within one skill guild to unlock the next tier for that guild. In Free Roam Mode, every quest is open from the start.

6. The Colorado Schoolhouse Tracker

Parents, use the Schoolhouse tab to track attendance and hours toward Colorado's homeschool requirements (172 days, 688 hours across 11 subjects). Every quest you complete can also count toward real learning.
