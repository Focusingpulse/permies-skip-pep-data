Welcome, Family.

You are about to embark on a journey that bridges the digital world with the dirt beneath your feet. This is not a game you play by staring at a screen; it is a game you play by living real life.

1. Competence, Not Just Points

In most games, you click a button and your character levels up. In The Village RPG, you are the character. To earn badges and upgrade your avatar, you must build real-world skills. The real-world reward is competence. The digital reward is watching your avatar evolve.

2. The Team Roster

Every quest requires a team effort. When you complete a task, you assign roles. Multiple people can hold the same role! If three kids are all scrubbing dishes, they are all Trackers.

  • Tracker(s): The people doing the physical labor.
  • Witness(es): The people catching the story (photos, asking "What did you notice?").
  • Hearth Tender(s): The people holding the space (making tea, prepping tools).
  • Mentor(s): The parent or guide who asks coyote questions — never gives the answer directly.

3. The Coyote Mentoring Rule

Parents, do not give your kids the answers. If the quest asks, "Why does hot water melt fat?", do not hand them a textbook. Ask them, "I wonder why the cold water made the grease hard?" Let them sit with the question.

4. The Otis Verification Protocol

To get officially certified on Permies.com, follow the Otis Protocol for photos:

  • Before: Raw materials / site.
  • During: You in the act of working.
  • After: Finished product WITH a physical sign showing your name and date.

5. Badge Progression

Quests are organized into four tiers of increasing skill: Sand (beginner) → Straw → Wood → Iron (master). Complete 50% of a tier's quests to unlock the next tier.
